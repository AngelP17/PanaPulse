## What the supplied montage demonstrates

1. **White wellness app:** a huge numeric focal point, airy surrounding information, clean spaced actions, an expressive circular visualization, and careful secondary labels. Strong information hierarchy is the real feature, **not the cyan gauge**.
2. **White achievement/plan panel:** rewards are treated as a polished editorial moment; progress is easy to scan, with one contrasting CTA. Borrow the sense of acknowledgment, not the exact product framing.
3. **Lavender assistant/card:** one memorable visual object and sparse supporting text. The value is distinct personality and an obvious next action, **not a generic purple AI mascot**.
4. **Near-black assistant:** a single layered, luminous focal surface in deep negative space. The value is controlled drama and depth; do not turn every PanaPulse utility status into a glowing orb.

## Motion and depth budget

- Keep existing theme crossfade and screen transitions; no broad animation-library migration.
- On selection, use a subtle 150–250ms press/selection response; honor Reduce Motion.
- Source-data numbers, origin/destination, community reports and timestamps **must not drift, loop or animate as if physically measured**.
- One expressive flourish per major flow is enough: onboarding completion, saved commute, or report-saved feedback. Never animate official danger states as entertainment.
- Distinct **light and dark designs**, not merely inverted colors. Continue using existing approved palettes rather than copying reference cyan, violet or pitch black.
- All interactive controls stay 44pt+ with visible focus/selected states, correct touch feedback and no hidden app navigation.
