3. **Atmospheric product vignette:** restrained porcelain/ember space frames one compact, clearly labeled feature preview, with one primary CTA.

Create sketch/renders of all three at the **same device dimensions**, ES and EN, in light and dark where applicable. Compare them against the current approved implementation, choose on usability and distinctiveness, **then** implement one chosen approach in React Native. A different iPhone font is not a reason to abandon the approved Manrope typeface. Do not confuse a web render with native device evidence.
