# PanaPulse brand v0.2.1

**Brand essence:** Panama, connected every day. Calm, useful, precise.

## Logo

- Brandmark: `assets/brandmark.svg` (connected-path `P`)
- Wordmarks: `assets/wordmark-light.svg` / `assets/wordmark-dark.svg`
- App icon: `assets/icon.png` / Android `assets/android-icon-foreground.png`
- Core colors: `#102635` navy, `#20C9DB` cyan, `#F9FCFD` porcelain

For marketing, preserve clear space around the P equal to at least one quarter of its height. Do not skew it, add glows, embed other symbols, recolor status icons with brand cyan, or use a bright cyan wordmark over similarly light backgrounds.

## Application interface

The home background may blend navy, bay-blue/teal, and a small amount of warm dusk color for atmosphere. Cyan is for branding/interaction, **not** for verification. Verification colors and labels remain independent and semantic (`src/theme/tokens.ts`).

Cards are intentionally large and rounded. Avoid shrinking text to cram data into uniform boxes. The four-tab bottom navigation is the product's own visual grammar, distinct from Hark's conversation-first nav. Status labels must always have textual meaning.

## Assets and provenance

These are bespoke concept assets, not trademark-cleared. Check wordmark and mark availability before public launch. Do not treat the logo-presentation image as a source asset; use the SVG/PNG files in `assets/`.
